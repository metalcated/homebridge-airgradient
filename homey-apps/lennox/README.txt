Connect compatible Lennox iComfort thermostats directly over your local network. See each zone's temperature, humidity and operating state, review temperature history in Insights, and use thermostat readings in Flows.

Add a thermostat using its local IP address. New devices start in read-only mode; disable Read-only mode in the device's advanced settings when you want Homey to change temperatures, modes or fan settings. Your existing Lennox schedules remain under the thermostat's control unless you send a command. Lennox cloud-only thermostats are not supported by this local integration.
